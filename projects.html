DOCTORAL RESEARCH · 2025–PRESENT

Epigenetic Mechanisms in Neurodegenerative Disease

University of Toronto · Zovkic Lab

Research Focus

My doctoral research investigates how epigenetic mechanisms influence neuronal function and contribute to neurodegenerative disease. I am particularly interested in the relationship between chromatin regulation, cellular homeostasis, and molecular changes associated with Alzheimer's disease.

Approach

I use an interdisciplinary approach that integrates molecular biology, neuroscience, and genomic techniques to study how changes in gene regulation affect brain function. My work combines experimental and computational methods to explore the molecular mechanisms underlying neurodegeneration.

Significance

Understanding how epigenetic regulation contributes to neuronal health and disease may provide new insights into the molecular processes associated with neurodegeneration.
